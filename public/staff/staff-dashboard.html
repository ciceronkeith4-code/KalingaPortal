Healthcare Staff Guide

Standard operating procedures for managing barangay residents, issuing prescriptions, dispensing medicine, and coordinating clinical consultations.

01

Patient Directory & Full Record Inspection

Access complete health records and manage active vs archived resident statuses.

  • Click Any Patient: Click any patient row in the registry to open their comprehensive profile, including medical prescription history and past consultation dates.
  • Archive / Restore Account: Temporarily suspend portal access for relocated residents using the Archive button. Re-activate anytime using Restore without losing past medical history.
  • Permanent Changes: For account deletions or password resets, coordinate directly with the Barangay Administrator.
02

Prescriptions & Dispensary Management

Issue new medications and fulfill resident refill requests.

  • Issuing Prescriptions: Select an active patient from the dropdown, specify medication name, instructions, and dosage notes, then submit.
  • Fulfilling Refill Requests: When residents submit medication requests, verify physical inventory in the Barangay 603 pharmacy and click Fulfill to notify the resident.
03

Schedule & Interactive Google Calendar

Coordinate clinic doctor visits, manage appointments, and confirm booking requests.

  • Interactive Calendar: View consultation dates on the monthly Google Calendar grid with color-coded status chips (Green for Confirmed, Amber for Requested).
  • Click-to-View Appointments: Click any appointment chip or schedule row to open the complete appointment modal. Staff can confirm or cancel directly from the modal.
  • Quick Date Selection: Click any date square on the calendar grid to automatically prefill the consultation date into the booking form.
  • View Switcher: Switch between Google Calendar Month Grid and Table List view using the top-right toggle buttons.
04

Common Staff Questions

Can health staff book appointments for walk-in residents?

Yes. Select the patient in the Schedule form, set the date and time, input consultation reason, and click "Set Appointment". The appointment will be saved immediately.

What happens when an appointment is confirmed?

The appointment status changes from Requested to Confirmed, updating immediately in both the staff schedule and the resident's personal portal.

How do I toggle the compact sidebar?

Click "Collapse Sidebar" above your profile card to collapse the navigation into an icon rail, maximizing screen real estate for patient charts.
