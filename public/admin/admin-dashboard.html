Administrator Guide

A concise operational reference for managing resident registrations, clinic schedule oversight, and portal access in Barangay 603.

01

Dashboard Overview & System Statistics

Monitor community healthcare coverage and appointment flow across Barangay 603 in real time.

  • Active vs. Archived Counts: Review total resident records and temporarily paused profiles at a glance.
  • Scheduled Consultations: Track total upcoming clinic visits booked through the community portal.
  • Live Activity: Monitor recently processed registrations and system updates.
02

Patient Directory & Account Management

Maintain accurate resident records, configure login credentials, and handle profile inquiries.

  • Click to View Profile: Click any patient row to open their full file, complete address, prescription records, and consultation history.
  • Account Registration: Click "Add Patient" to register a verified resident with their complete home address and temporary password.
  • Editing & Password Resets: Click "Edit" on a patient row to update contact information or reset a forgotten password.
  • Permanent Deletion: Use the "Delete" action solely for duplicate accounts or expunged entries. For temporary absences, health staff should use "Archive" instead.
03

Schedule & Interactive Google Calendar

Explore and monitor appointments via month grid or tabular list view.

  • Google Calendar View: View all scheduled doctor visits plotted on the interactive monthly grid.
  • Click-to-View Appointments: Click any appointment chip on the calendar to view patient details, reason for visit, and consultation status.
  • Navigation & Switching: Use the previous/next arrows and "Today" button to move through dates, or toggle to Table List view anytime.
04

Common Administrative Questions

How do I assist a resident who forgot their password?

Open the Patients table, find the resident, click "Edit", input their new password into the password field, and save. The change applies immediately.

What is the distinction between Archive and Delete?

Archiving pauses resident login access while preserving all medical and prescription history. Deletion permanently purges the user and cannot be undone.

How do I use the sidebar collapse feature?

Click "Collapse Sidebar" above your profile card to collapse the navigation into an icon rail, giving full width to calendar and tables.
