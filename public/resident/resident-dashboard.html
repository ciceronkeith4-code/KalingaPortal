Resident & Patient Portal Guide

A simple guide to accessing your Barangay 603 healthcare services: requesting free medications, booking doctor consultations, and reviewing your health records.

01

Health Overview & Personal Details

Check your community health metrics and registered profile on file.

  • Status Cards: Your dashboard shows your total active prescriptions and upcoming doctor consultations at a glance.
  • My Details: Open "My Details" to view your registered official name, age, home address, and username. Inform the Barangay Health Center if your address changes.
02

Requesting Free Barangay Medications

Submit medicine requests and track when they are ready for dispensary pickup.

  • Submitting a Request: Go to "Prescriptions", enter your medication name and instructions or dosage needs, and submit. The status will display as amber Requested.
  • Dispensary Pickup: Once health staff process and prepare your medicine, the status switches to green Fulfilled. Visit the health center counter with a valid ID to claim it.
03

Consultation Calendar & Booking Visits

Schedule clinic appointments with the health center doctor using the interactive calendar.

  • Interactive Google Calendar: Your consultation schedule is displayed on an interactive monthly calendar. Click any appointment chip or schedule row to view visit details.
  • Booking an Appointment: Click any day on the calendar or fill out the booking form with your desired date, preferred time, and reason for consultation.
  • Confirmation: Health staff will confirm your slot, changing its status to green Confirmed. Arrive 10 minutes prior to your scheduled time.
04

Frequently Asked Questions

Are consultations and medications free for residents?

Yes. All medical consultations and dispensary medications provided by Barangay 603 are completely free of charge for registered residents.

What should I bring when claiming my fulfilled medicines?

Bring any valid ID (Barangay ID, Government ID, or Student ID) and your doctor's prescription slip if applicable.

How do I collapse the sidebar for more screen space?

Click the "Collapse Sidebar" button above your profile card to toggle into compact mode anytime.
